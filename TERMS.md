# Terms of Service — TriageGenie

Last updated: 2026-11-16

TriageGenie is a Forge app for Jira, built as an entry for Atlassian Codegeist 2026 by kiki ki. The app is provided free of charge, "as is", without warranty of any kind.

## Use

The app classifies Jira work items, suggests a priority, labels, an assignee, a draft first response, and a fix plan. All suggestions are generated automatically — please review them before acting on them. The app never posts, assigns, or changes anything without being triggered by an issue being created or by a user clicking Apply in the panel.
