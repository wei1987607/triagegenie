# Privacy Policy — TriageGenie

Last updated: 2026-11-16

TriageGenie is a Forge app for Jira, built as an entry for Atlassian Codegeist 2026 by kiki ki.

## What the app accesses

When you triage a Jira work item (automatically on creation, or from the TriageGenie issue panel, or through the Rovo agent), the app reads the data of that work item — its summary, description, comments, labels, assignee, and related team data through Atlassian's Teamwork Graph — in order to compute a category, a priority score, suggested labels, a suggested assignee, a draft first response, and a fix plan.

## What the app stores

TriageGenie does **not** store personal data. It has no database and uses no persistent storage. Triage results are written back to the Jira issue itself (labels, priority, assignee, and a comment) only when the app applies them, and only on the site where the app is installed.

## What the app shares

Nothing. The app sends no data to any third party, runs no analytics, and makes no network calls outside Atlassian's own APIs.
